1. Introduction

FITKEY (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the mobile application FITKEY - Gym & Fitness Pass (Package: com.fitkey.fitkey_user). FITKEY provides flexible access to multi-venue partner gyms, swimming pools, yoga studios, and combat centres with single memberships, instant QR code check-in, on-demand video workouts, and fitness streak tracking.

2. Data We Collect & Usage

We collect only the information necessary to provide authentication, membership verification, and workout logging:

3. Device Permissions Declared

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for secure communication with FITKEY cloud APIs over encrypted HTTPS.
  • android.permission.CAMERA: Required exclusively to scan QR codes displayed at gym entrances for contactless check-in. The camera is never accessed in the background or for recording.

4. In-App Purchases & Payment Security

FITKEY is free to download; memberships, passes, and fitness plans are purchased in-app. All financial transactions are securely handled by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay, Stripe, Google Play Billing). We never store payment card numbers, CVVs, or banking PINs.

5. Health & Medical Disclaimer

FITKEY is a workout tracking and venue access pass tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, or clinical health advice. Consult your physician before initiating any vigorous physical training program.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

Users have the complete right to permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App: Profile > Settings > Account Management > Delete Account.
  • Web Deletion Portal: Visit our dedicated web portal to delete your data without installing the app:
Open FITKEY Account Deletion Portal →

Upon request, all user details, attendance records, check-in history, and photo uploads will be permanently erased from our databases within 30 days.

7. Data Security & Encryption

All communications between the FITKEY app and our servers use TLS 1.3 encryption. We employ strict access controls to safeguard data against unauthorized disclosure.

8. Children's Privacy

FITKEY is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
