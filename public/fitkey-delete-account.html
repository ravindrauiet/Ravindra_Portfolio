Google Play Data Safety Requirement

Request FITKEY Account & Data Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your FITKEY - Gym & Fitness Pass (Package: com.fitkey.fitkey_user) account and erase your personal records, use any of the options below.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Quickest)

1. Open the FITKEY app.
2. Tap Profile > Settings > Account Management.
3. Tap Delete Account and confirm with OTP.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request Form (Without Reinstalling App)

Per Google Play Store requirements, you can request account deletion directly from this browser page:

Deletion Request Logged

Our security team will permanently delete your FITKEY profile, gym check-in records, and attendance history within 30 days. A confirmation SMS will be dispatched.

Warning: Deleting your account will immediately forfeit any active gym passes and erase your check-in streak history. This action is irreversible.

Option 3: Request via Email

You can also send a request directly from your email to our support team:

Email Support (support@fitkey.in)
Data Retention & Erasure Policy:
• Personal profile data, name, phone number, and attendance check-in records are wiped within 30 days.
• Statutory tax invoices for past purchases may be securely archived as required by Indian taxation laws.
