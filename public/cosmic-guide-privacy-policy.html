All data transmitted between the CosmicGuide mobile app and our backend servers is encrypted with TLS/HTTPS. We follow industry best practices to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety requirements and user privacy laws, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your CosmicGuide account and all associated personal data at any time without needing to reinstall the app.

Data Deletion Breakdown:

  • Retained Data: None. All user records tied to your identity are erased. Anonymous, aggregated diagnostic crash logs may be kept strictly for system performance.
