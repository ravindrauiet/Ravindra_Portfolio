Scene two · The fold

How a momo is made.

12pleats
folded
01

Dough

Maida, a pinch of salt and warm water, kneaded smooth and rested for an hour. Each ball is rolled thin at the edges and a little thicker in the middle, so the base holds up to the steam.

02

Filling

Cabbage, onion, carrot, ginger and plenty of garlic, chopped by hand each morning and seasoned with a little soy and spring onion. Chicken filling is minced fresh and kept in its own station.

03

Pleat

A spoon of filling, then the fold: thumb holds, finger tucks, twelve times round until the top closes into a little knot. Our fastest cook folds a plate of eight in under two minutes.

04

Steam

Into oiled bamboo baskets, stacked three high over a rolling boil. Eight minutes for veg, ten for chicken, then straight to your table with the chutney still warm.
