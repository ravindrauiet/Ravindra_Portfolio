The wedding day

Five chapters, one thread

Indian weddings run for days. We plan coverage around every ritual, so nothing important happens while we are changing lenses.

Morning · Chapter one

Haldi

turmeric & laughter

Yellow everywhere, family everywhere. We shoot fast and close on quiet primes, so the giggles stay real and nobody is asked to pose.

  • 2 photographers
  • Candid only
Afternoon · Chapter two

Mehendi

patterns that tell stories

Long, unhurried hours of henna and songs. Macro details for the album, slow-motion clips for the film, and the hidden initials found.

  • Macro details
  • Slow-motion film
Evening · Chapter three

Sangeet

the night of music

Rehearsed numbers, unrehearsed dancing. Two cameras on the stage, one roaming the floor, and stage lighting we plan with your decorator.

  • Multi-camera
  • Live sound
The muhurat · Chapter four

Pheras

seven promises

The most important hour, filmed with the most respect. We agree positions with your pandit beforehand and never step into the mandap.

  • Silent shutters
  • Pre-agreed angles
Night · Chapter five

Reception

the grand finale

Décor before the doors open, portraits in the last light, speeches, the first dance and every auntie on the dance floor.

  • Décor shoot
  • Same-night teaser
