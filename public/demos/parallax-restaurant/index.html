Our name ZAFRAAN

Saffron, in Hindi and Persian. The most precious spice in the kitchen.

Every menu at Zafraan begins with a spice, not a dish. We travel to Pampore for saffron, to Idukki for cardamom and to the hills of Guntur for chillies, and we cook only what those harvests give us.

The result is an Indian tasting menu that changes with the seasons: familiar flavours from home kitchens, cooked with fine-dining precision and served slowly across one long evening.

The chef

Meher Kapoor cooks the food her grandmother wrote down.

After a decade in kitchens in London and Copenhagen, Meher came home with one notebook: her nani's handwritten recipes from Amritsar, Lucknow and Kozhikode. Zafraan is that notebook, cooked with everything she learned along the way.

Her kitchen of twelve grinds every masala by hand each morning, bakes its own breads in a wood-fired tandoor and works directly with forty farming families.

Spice is memory. My job is to make you remember a place you have never been.Chef Meher Kapoor
