    The atelier

    Atelier

    Every piece is drawn, cut and stitched by forty-two artisans in a sunlit studio in Bandra. Slowly, by hand, and in small numbers.

    01
    01 · Design

    It begins with a pencil

    Each silhouette starts as a sketch on the studio table, pinned, redrawn and fitted on a form until the line feels effortless.

    02 · Fabric

    Cloth chosen by touch

    Cotton, linen, wool and suede, sourced in small lots and washed in-house so every piece feels lived-in from the first wear.

    03 · Cut

    Cut one at a time

    Patterns are laid by hand and cut with shears, never stacked, so stripes meet at the seams and the grain falls true.

    04 · Stitch

    Finished by hand

    Machine seams, hand-finished hems and buttonholes. A blazer takes eighteen hours; we sign the inside label when it is done.
