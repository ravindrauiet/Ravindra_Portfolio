CHERRY→CUP

From cherry
to cup

01 / 05
  1. Step 01 · Nov to Feb

    Picked only when red

    Pickers on shade-grown estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg hand-pick only the deep-red, fully ripe coffee cherries.

  2. Step 02 · 12 to 18 days

    Pulped and sun-dried

    The fruit is pulped away and the seeds are dried on raised beds until they become pale green coffee beans.

  3. Step 03 · drop at 218°C

    Roasted in small batches

    In our Mumbai roastery the green beans turn yellow, then cinnamon, then chocolate, then a glossy dark brown.

  4. Step 04 · minutes before

    Ground to order

    We grind on steel burrs just before brewing: coarse for cold brew and pour-over, fine for espresso.

  5. Step 05 · 18-hour steep

    Poured slow, into your cup

    Cold brew steeps overnight and hot coffee is poured by hand. Either way, it ends here, in your cup.
