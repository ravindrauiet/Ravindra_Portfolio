The studio

A boutique developer building fewer, better homes on the edge of the Aravali ridge since 2008.

We build slowly and on purpose: deep verandas against the Haryana sun, stone and lime that stay cool in May, and gardens planted before the first wall goes up. Every home is drawn around the light it will receive.

02

Timber, lime and soft light.

Interiors in teak, hand-applied lime plaster and Jaisalmer stone. Rooms are lit from two sides and every bedroom has a window seat facing the green.

  • Teak joinery
  • Lime plaster
  • Window seats

Roofs that shade, walls that breathe.

Deep overhangs keep the summer sun off the glass, cross-ventilation runs through every room and solar panels sit quietly on the roof.

  • Cross-ventilated
  • Rooftop solar
03

Every home opens to the outdoors.

A garden court, a terrace or a planted balcony with every residence, plus forty acres of protected ridge forest right beyond the boundary wall.
