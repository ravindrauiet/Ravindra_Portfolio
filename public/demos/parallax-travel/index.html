Sample itinerary

Kedarkantha in six days

A winter summit that suits first-timers with good fitness. Short walking days, a rest-friendly pace and a 3 am start for the summit sunrise.

3,810 m summit20 km on footEasy–moderateDehradun pickup
Day 01

Dehradun to Sankri

Pickup at 6:30 am and a scenic drive along the Tons river to the trailhead village of Sankri. Gear check and briefing in the evening.

210 km drive1,950 m
Day 02

Into the pine forest

A gentle climb through pine and oak to Juda ka Talab, a lake that freezes solid in winter. Our first camp sits right beside it.

4 km · 5 hrs2,800 m
Day 03

Kedarkantha base

The forest opens into wide snow meadows. Afternoon is free for acclimatisation walks and a lesson in using microspikes.

4 km · 4 hrs3,400 m
Day 04

Summit day

Headtorches on at 3 am. Reach the top for sunrise over Swargarohini, then descend through the snow to the camp at Hargaon.

9 km · 9 hrs3,810 m
Day 05

Back to Sankri

An easy walk down past shepherd huts and apple orchards. Hot showers, a bonfire and the trek certificates in the evening.

6 km · 4 hrs1,950 m
Day 06

Home with a summit

Breakfast and the drive back to Dehradun, arriving by 6 pm in time for evening trains and flights.

210 km driveDrop at station
