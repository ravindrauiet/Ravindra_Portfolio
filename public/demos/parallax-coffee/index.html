01 / 04
Harvest
01 · Harvest

Picked ripe, one cherry at a time.

Our partner farmers in the Baba Budangiri hills grow arabica under silver oak and pepper vines. Only fully red cherries are picked, by hand, over several passes.

1,450 m altitudeShade grownHand picked
02 · Process

Washed, sun-dried and sorted.

The cherries are pulped, fermented and dried on raised beds for up to three weeks, then the green beans are sorted by eye before they travel to Bengaluru.

Washed & natural lots21-day drying
03 · Roast

Roasted at six, every morning.

Each lot gets its own roast profile on our 12 kg drum roaster. We cup every batch before it is bagged, and nothing sits on the shelf for more than three weeks.

12 kg batchesCupped dailyRoast date on every bag
04 · Pour

Poured for you, just so.

At the bar, every shot is weighed and timed and every pour-over is brewed to order, so the cup in your hands tastes exactly as the farmer intended.
