Build a Margherita

Four things.
No shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Dough · 48 h

    Flour, water, salt and a pinch of yeast, rested for two days so the crust puffs light and blistered.

  2. 02

    San Marzano sauce

    Hand-crushed tomatoes and sea salt. Spread thin, never cooked twice.

  3. 03

    Fior di latte

    Fresh cow's-milk mozzarella, torn by hand into soft clouds.

  4. 04

    Fresh basil

    Picked that morning, laid on just before the fire.
