The splash zone

Wear white. Leave yellow.

Once the bride and groom are done, nobody is safe. Cousins carry bowls, aunties carry opinions and every white kurta ends the day a little more golden.

  • Wear cotton you don't mind staining forever.
  • Leave the silk and the white sneakers at home.
  • Turmeric washes off skin with besan and milk, not soap.
  • Phones in pockets: the splash zone has no mercy.

Dress code

Sunshine colours, comfortable cotton

Think yellow, white and leaf green. Loose cotton, chikankari, mulmul and flat sandals. You will be sitting on the floor and dancing on grass.

Good to know: we'll have spare white kurtas and dupattas at the door for anyone who'd rather not ruin their own.
